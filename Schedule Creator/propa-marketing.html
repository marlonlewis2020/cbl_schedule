Good to know

A few quick answers.

How do I get started?

Register your business with a BambooHR subdomain and API key. Sign in to set up your authenticator and check your approval status.

Can my employees see their shifts?

Silver and above support employee schedule access and shift trades. Managers and employees use their workforce sign-in.

Where do I manage membership?

Your business administrator can view membership status. The primary admin can manage billing in the workspace when billing is available.
