Definitions, scope and responsible use

Counts represent shift records, not unique employees. The snapshot contains shifts already started on its reporting day plus verified overnight carry-in. Full-day scheduled totals and future shifts are not available in this saved scope. Approved leave is excluded from expected shifts and attendance percentage.

Confirmed attendance respects saved attendance decisions and worked-time validation: attended records are confirmed; late records additionally require a matching clock-in and at least 60 worked minutes (or the full shift for shorter shifts). A late status without a clock-in is a missing punch, not a confirmed arrival. Open shifts awaiting the worked-time minimum remain unconfirmed. Saved absence and correction decisions are not reclassified by this report.

Missing clock-outs are flagged for follow-up only after a known shift end. Open shifts are not automatically missing-clock-out incidents. Early departure and overtime minutes are the saved rule results. Unknown shift ends/worked time stay unavailable. No fabricated trends, comparisons, demand forecasts or automatic emails are included.

Filters affect this local view only; the captured data never changes. A downloaded report contains employee information: share only with authorized recipients. Downloaded copies cannot be revoked when your app session expires.
